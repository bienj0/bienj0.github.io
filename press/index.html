About Appy Birthday

Get a heads-up while there's still time to do something about it, keep every gift idea in one place, and send a card nobody else could have made. No account. No ads. No data collected.

  • Reminders that give you time – Choose per person when you get a heads-up: a week ahead for Mom's gift, the morning of for a coworker.
  • Pin gifts from any website – Spotted the perfect gift online? Tap Share, choose Appy Birthday, and it lands straight on their Birthday Board. Works for wishlists and invitations too.
  • The birthday board – Every gift idea, wishlist and invitation for everyone, neatly in one place.
  • Cards only they could get – Create a one-of-a-kind birthday card based on what they love, made with Apple's Image Playground. Share it in a few taps.
  • Widgets for your home screen – See who's next at a glance. Make a card or message right from your Home Screen, without opening the app.
  • All your birthdays in seconds – Import them from Contacts or add them one by one, with or without the year.
  • On iPhone and iPad – Plan every birthday on the big screen too.
  • Make it yours – Choose from themes, backgrounds and app icons.
  • Private by design – We collect nothing. Premium backups stay in your own private iCloud.
